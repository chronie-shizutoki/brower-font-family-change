How It Works

Targeted Replacement

Only replaces fonts like Microsoft YaHei, DengXian, MiSans, PingFang, etc. with system-ui. Other fonts are left untouched.

Icon Protection

Font Awesome, Material Icons, Google Symbols, and other icon fonts are automatically preserved.

Dynamic Content

Monitors DOM changes and applies replacement to newly added elements in real-time.

Multi-language

Supports Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, English, Japanese, and Korean interfaces.
